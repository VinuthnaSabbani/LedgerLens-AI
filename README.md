## 💡 What is LedgerLens AI?

Online merchants can lose revenue due to payment failures, refund spikes, and settlement issues.

LedgerLens AI helps merchants answer:

**What happened? → Why? → How much money is at risk? → What should we do? → Did it work?**

It turns financial anomalies into actionable incidents through an AI-powered workflow.

---

## 🔄 Core Workflow

**Detect → Investigate → Quantify → Simulate → Act → Measure → Learn**

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📊 **Financial Dashboard** — Revenue, money at risk, money recovered, active incidents and profit trends.
- 🚨 **Incident Detection** — Identifies payment failures, refund surges and settlement issues.
- 🔍 **AI Investigation** — Explains the likely root cause, evidence and financial impact.
- 💰 **Impact Analysis** — Translates financial incidents into estimated monetary exposure.
- 🔮 **What-If Simulator** — Estimates how earlier intervention could have changed the outcome.
- ⚙️ **Actions & Audit** — Tracks recommended actions, approvals and financial outcomes.
- 📈 **Postmortems** — Captures incident timelines, recovery results and prevention steps.
- 🧪 **Test Environment** — Simulates financial incidents using synthetic data.
- 🛡️ **Action Guardrails** — Approval requirements, action limits and audit tracking.

Traditional dashboards tell merchants:
