1. Plain-English Summary

On August 31, 2026, at 2:14 PM, card payments started failing because HDFC Bank's server slowed down. 1,247 customer checkout orders were affected, putting ₹4,20,000 at risk. LedgerLens AI immediately alerted you and offered shoppers a 1-tap Google Pay / PhonePe option. This quick action saved ₹1,80,000 in customer sales that would have otherwise walked away.

2. Why It Happened (In Simple Terms)

The bank's computer was taking over 14 seconds to respond instead of the normal 1 second, causing customer checkouts to time out.

Main Contributing Factors:
  • Too many card payments were relying on a single bank partner at the same time.
  • Customers trying repeatedly to pay made the bank's line longer before we switched them to UPI.

3. Safety Rules Put in Place for Next Time

Automatic Protection Rules

  • Rule 1: Automatically switch to backup bank (ICICI / Axis) if card delays exceed 3 minutes.
  • Rule 2: Instantly show 1-click UPI to shoppers if their card takes longer than 4 seconds.
  • Rule 3: Never send more than 60% of traffic to a single bank to prevent all eggs in one basket.
